Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective: August 24, 2026

Your privacy is fundamental to HashGANG Chat. This Privacy Policy details how we protect your personal information when using our anonymous stranger video chat platform (https://chat.hashgang.com).

1. Zero Video & Text Message Storage

HashGANG Chat utilizes WebRTC (Web Real-Time Communication) peer-to-peer streaming technology. Video streams, microphone audio, and text chat messages are transmitted directly between participating browsers and are NEVER stored, recorded, or intercepted on any server.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in absolute anonymity. We do NOT collect or request:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or social media accounts.
  • Account passwords or personal identification numbers.
  • Video recordings, call histories, or chat transcript logs.

3. Technical Data Collected Automatically

To deliver matchmaking, prevent platform abuse, and serve targeted ad mediation, our backend API processes standard anonymous technical metrics:

  • Anonymized Visitor ID: A randomly generated browser string saved in your device's localStorage to maintain session preferences.
  • Cryptographic IP Hashes: IP addresses are hashed using SHA-256 for rate limiting and abuse prevention (banning reported bad actors). Raw IP addresses are not stored in analytics.
  • Device Category & Geo Country Code: Non-precise general device type (mobile/desktop) and ISO country code for regional matchmaking and ad mediation.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use tracking cookies for cross-site profiling. We use standard browser localStorage for essential app functionality:

  • Storing your Terms of Service consent status.
  • Remembering UI theme preferences (Dark / Light mode).
  • Maintaining a local list of blocked peer IDs so you are never re-matched with blocked users.

5. Third-Party Advertising & Dynamic Mediation

To keep HashGANG Chat 100% free, we utilize dynamic waterfall ad mediation (such as Adsterra, PropellerAds, Monetag, and Self-Brand promotions). Third-party ad networks may place standard non-personalized ad banners. No personal chat data is ever shared with advertising networks.

6. Children's Privacy

HashGANG Chat is strictly intended for individuals who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect or permit data from minors. If you believe a minor is accessing the service, please report it to us immediately.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or data protection, please email us at: info@hashgang.com.
