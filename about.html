About HashGANG Chat

A zero-cost, high-scale, decentralized stranger chat and multiplayer gaming platform engineered for safe, fast, and authentic human connections worldwide.

Our Mission & Genesis

Following the closure of legacy stranger video chat services in late 2023, the global internet community was left with fragmented alternatives that quickly became cluttered with expensive coin paywalls, automated bot spam, intrusive trackers, and compromised user privacy.

HashGANG Chat was built to redefine the stranger chat experience from the ground up: 100% free forever, requiring no phone numbers or personal credentials, powered by modern peer-to-peer (P2P) WebRTC technology, and designed with safety, transparency, and respect as our foundational principles.

Whether you want to practice speaking a new language, share laughs with peers halfway across the globe, engage in an instant multiplayer Ludo board game match, or chill in the group Public Lounge, HashGANG delivers instant, high-framerate social interactions without friction.

Architectural Pillars & Technology

HashGANG Chat is engineered with a modern, high-performance tech stack prioritizing decentralization and user privacy:

Decentralized P2P Mesh
All 1v1 video, audio, and gameplay communications flow directly peer-to-peer using end-to-end encrypted DTLS/SRTP WebRTC protocols. No media passes through or is recorded on central relay servers.
Real-Time Multiplayer Ludo
Play real-time 2-player, 3-player, and 4-player Ludo matches right inside your chat window, synchronized over P2P WebRTC data channels with integrated voice chat and authentic sound feedback.
Client-Side WebAssembly DSP
Real-time voice pitch modification (Chipmunk, Deep, Robot) powered by WebAssembly (Rubber Band & SoundTouch AudioWorklet) running 100% locally in your browser with zero latency.
Public Lounge Sharding
Dynamic 25-user public lounge shards enable lively community discussions, icebreaker questions, and casual hangouts with zero-friction instant access.
AI Virtual Backgrounds & Filters
Client-side privacy background blurs, vibrant virtual backgrounds, and Gen-Z Neon Aura Glow shaders keep conversations visually delightful while protecting your physical surroundings.
100% Free • No Hidden Coins
We never charge users for matches, filters, or games. HashGANG is funded purely through ethical, non-intrusive, family-safe advertisements and internal SaaS cross-promotions.

Trust, Safety & Community Standards

We maintain an uncompromising stance against harassment, predatory conduct, and illegal behavior. Our multi-tier safety architecture includes:

  • Strict 18+ Adult Policy: HashGANG Chat is strictly restricted to adults aged 18 and older. Minors are strictly prohibited from using the platform.
  • Ephemeral Sessions & Zero Data Storage: Text messages and video streams exist only in browser memory during an active session and are permanently cleared the moment either party disconnects.
  • Session Trace Watermarking: Every active video stream incorporates a centered, dynamic cryptographic Session Trace Code watermark to strongly deter non-consensual screen recording or extortion.
  • Instant 1-Click Report & Block: Users can immediately disconnect from and permanently block any stranger who behaves inappropriately, with automated incident logging for law enforcement cooperation when legally mandated.
  • No Bots Allowed: Intelligent rate limiters, WebSocket handshake validation, and proof-of-work safeguards actively eliminate automated spam bots and fraudulent traffic.
