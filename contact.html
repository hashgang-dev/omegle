Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a user violating community guidelines?
Click the red Report & Disconnect button in your active chat. This immediately severs the peer-to-peer connection, adds the offender to your personal blocklist, and securely logs the active session watermark trace code for moderation review.
Can HashGANG recover chat logs or video recordings?
No. HashGANG operates under a strict Zero-Knowledge and Zero-Storage architecture. Video and audio streams flow directly peer-to-peer via encrypted DTLS/SRTP WebRTC protocols and are never stored on our servers.
How can I manage my ad preferences and cookies?
We partner with Google AdSense and trusted ad networks for family-safe, non-adult ads. You can manage personalized advertising preferences anytime via Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.
