2. How to Install on Android (Chrome WebAPK)

On Android devices running Google Chrome, installing HashGANG Chat is a one-tap process that automatically compiles a lightweight WebAPK:

1 Open the Website

Open Google Chrome on your Android phone and navigate to chat.hashgang.com.

2 Tap the Install Banner or Chrome Menu

You will see an instant "Install HashGANG Chat App" prompt at the top of your screen. Alternatively, tap Chrome's three-dot menu () in the top-right corner and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

3 Confirm Installation

Tap Install. In less than 3 seconds, a native HashGANG icon will appear on your home screen and in your Android app drawer.

3. How to Install on iPhone & iPad (iOS Safari)

Apple's iOS (iOS 16.4 and newer) provides full support for standalone Progressive Web Apps and Web Push notifications directly through Mobile Safari:

1 Open Safari

Launch Safari on your iPhone or iPad and go to chat.hashgang.com.

2 Tap the Share Button

Tap the Share icon (the square with an arrow pointing upward) located on the bottom toolbar of Safari.

3 Select "Add to Home Screen"

Scroll down the share sheet and tap "Add to Home Screen" (), then tap Add in the top-right corner.

4 Launch and Allow Notifications

Tap the new HashGANG Chat icon on your iOS home screen. When prompted, tap Allow for notifications so you never miss a starred friend coming back online.

4. Enabling Background Notifications for Starred Friends

HashGANG Chat's standout feature is the Secret Star Favorites system, which alerts you when past chat partners return online. To ensure notifications wake your phone reliably:

  • Ensure Battery Optimization is Balanced: On Android, go to Settings > Apps > Chrome (or HashGANG Chat) > Battery and set it to Optimized or Unrestricted so background push alerts arrive instantly.
  • Keep the PWA Installed: Opening HashGANG through your installed home screen icon preserves your local cryptographic star tokens and notification subscriptions indefinitely.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does installing a PWA take up device storage?

Virtually none. While traditional store apps take 150 MB or more, HashGANG Chat PWA uses less than 1 MB of disk space.

Do I need to manually update the app when you release new features?

No. PWAs update automatically. Every time you launch the app, our service worker checks for the newest build and seamlessly updates in the background.

Can I uninstall the PWA whenever I want?

Yes. Long-press the icon on your Android or iPhone home screen and tap "Uninstall" or "Delete Bookmark" just like any normal mobile app.
