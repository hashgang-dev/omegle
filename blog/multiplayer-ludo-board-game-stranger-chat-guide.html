3. Fair Play, Privacy & Anti-Trolling Guardrails

Online gaming with strangers requires ironclad safety. HashGANG's Ludo engine was engineered with modern anti-abuse mechanisms:

  • Strict Resignation & Forfeit Handling: If a player disconnects or quits, the system cleanly awards victory or advances turns without freezing the board.
  • Floating Arena Minimizer: Players can minimize the Ludo board into a compact floating pill at any time to check lounge text messages without losing game state.
  • No Screen Relay / Zero Media Relaying: Audio is transmitted purely between connected peers via WebRTC encryption—your data never passes through intermediary third-party recording servers.
