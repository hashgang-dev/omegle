How to Fix Camera & Microphone Permission Denied Issues in Web Browsers

If you see a "Permission Denied" or "Camera/Microphone Not Found" warning when launching a video chat, don't worry! This guide provides simple steps to unblock camera access across all devices.

1. Google Chrome (Desktop & Android)

Chrome Desktop

Click the 🔒 lock or tune icon on the left side of the address bar -> Toggle Camera and Microphone to Allow -> Refresh the page.

Chrome Android

Open Chrome Settings -> Site Settings -> Camera & Microphone -> Ensure chat.hashgang.com is under Allowed.

2. iOS Safari (iPhone & iPad)

Safari Settings

Go to iPhone Settings -> Safari -> Camera & Microphone -> Set to Ask or Allow. Reload the web page.
