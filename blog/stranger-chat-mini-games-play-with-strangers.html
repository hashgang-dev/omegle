Positioned neatly above your group chat input pill, Pop Color Balloon transforms idle waiting time into high-energy entertainment. Target Color Matching

The top header displays your target color (e.g., YELLOW, RED, BLUE, or GREEN). Only tap balloons matching the active target to score points.

Streak Multipliers

  1. Score & Post: A user plays Pop Color Balloon and finishes with a high score (e.g., 150 points). On the summary overlay, they tap "Post Score in Group".
  2. In-Feed Challenge Bubble: A rich social card appears in the public Group Lounge feed with the player's score, level, and a glowing [ ⚡ Beat {Name}'s Score ] button.
  3. One-Tap Instant Revenge: Any stranger visiting the site (whether 10 seconds or 2 hours later) clicks the button. The game launches immediately with an active in-game target pill: 🎯 Beat: 150.
