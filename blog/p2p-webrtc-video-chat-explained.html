How WebRTC Direct Video Connections Protect Your Online Privacy

WebRTC (Web Real-Time Communication) is an open-source framework that enables high-definition audio, video, and data communication directly between browsers without intermediate media servers.

Server-Mediated vs Peer-to-Peer Architecture

Traditional video chat services route your video feed through central servers where video streams could theoretically be recorded or monitored. HashGANG Chat utilizes decentralized WebRTC connections:

  • Direct Browser Mesh: Video streams pass directly from Browser A to Browser B.
  • Zero Video Storage: Central servers only assist in initial handshake (STUN/ICE), never touching video data.
  • Low Latency: Direct connections eliminate relay delays for crisp 60 FPS video calls.
