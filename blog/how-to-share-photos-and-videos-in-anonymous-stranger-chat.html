2. How P2P Encrypted Media Transfer Protects Your Privacy

Many users wonder: "If I send a photo to a stranger, does HashGANG store my picture on central servers?"

The answer is an absolute NO. HashGANG Chat utilizes WebRTC RTCDataChannel technology. When you select an image or short video:

  • Direct Browser-to-Browser Transfer: Your media file is chunked into encrypted binary packets and sent directly from your browser to your partner's browser.
  • Zero Central File Servers: The image or video is **NEVER uploaded to or saved on any central server**. Once your chat session ends, the temporary memory buffer is cleared automatically.
  • No Cloud Traces: There are no persistent URL links or cloud storage databases holding your media files.
