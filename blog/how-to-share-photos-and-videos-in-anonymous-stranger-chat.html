1. Strictly View-Once by Default: Why Direct View Was Deprecated

On older chat apps, sent media remains pinned in the chat log indefinitely, creating immense anxiety:

  • What if someone walks behind your desk or sits next to you on public transit while a photo is visible?
  • What if a stranger saves, forwards, or re-inspects your photo minutes after the conversation moves on?

To solve this at the root architecture level, HashGANG Chat completely removed unshielded "Direct View" modes from private chat. Every single photo and video uploaded from your camera or gallery is strictly enforced as View-Once:

The Ephemeral View-Once Architecture:

  • Discreet Opaque Cards: Incoming and outgoing media render as discreet status cards in chat: Photo received • [View Photo] or Video received • [View Video]. Nothing is exposed on screen until the user intentionally clicks to view.
  • Single-Use Lifespan: Media can only ever be opened once. The instant the viewing session terminates, the card permanently updates to a grayed-out [1] Opened status. It cannot be re-opened, reloaded, or recovered.
  • Immediate RAM Garbage Collection: As soon as the modal closes, the underlying browser Object Blob URL is immediately revoked (URL.revokeObjectURL), and binary media chunks in device memory are wiped clean (isBurnedInMemory: true).

2. The HTML5 Forensic Lightbox: Touch-and-Hold & Full-Surface Scanner

When you tap "View Photo" or "View Video", media does not simply flash open. Instead, it renders in an isolated, dark forensic lightbox modal blurred behind a full-surface vertical laser scanner with an animated biometric fingerprint aura:

  • Continuous Touch-and-Hold Requirement: The user must press and hold down anywhere on the screen (Touch & Hold to View for photos, Touch & Hold to Play for videos). The media remains visible and unblurred only while physical contact with the display is maintained.
  • Photos (10-Second Countdown & Instant Release Destruction): While holding, an animated countdown ring counts down from 10s to 0. If the viewer lifts their finger at any time (even after 1 second), the photo immediately vanishes, burns from device RAM, and permanently closes.
  • Videos (Hold-to-Play & Natural Duration): View-Once videos play with full audio strictly while the screen is held. Every video frame is rendered onto an HTML5 <canvas> in real time with dynamic forensic watermarks. Releasing your finger pauses and destroys the video immediately; if held to the end, the video auto-destructs the millisecond playback concludes (hiddenVideo.onended).
  • Physical Defense Against OS Screenshots: Web browsers operate in secure sandboxes that cannot intercept operating system kernel button combinations (like Android/iOS Power + Volume Down). By requiring one hand to continuously hold the glass, taking a physical screenshot requires awkward, two-handed coordination. Moreover, multi-touch swipe shortcuts (like 3-finger swipe screenshot gestures) are actively detected by the browser touch engine (touches.length > 1), immediately triggering an emergency blackout curtain before a capture can occur!

4. Active Anti-Screenshot Hotkeys, Multi-Touch Gestures & Blackout Curtain

HashGANG Chat provides active, proactive client-side defenses while media is displayed in the forensic lightbox:

  • Multi-Touch Gesture Blackout: Android and iOS devices frequently support 3-finger or 2-finger swipe gestures for quick screenshots. HashGANG's touch engine listens to active touch count (touches.length > 1). The instant multiple touch points are detected, an emergency Security Blackout Curtain drops and the media is destroyed instantly.
  • Hotkey Interception: Pressing PrintScreen, Ctrl+P (Print), or Ctrl+S (Save Page) immediately triggers the opaque Security Blackout Curtain, overwrites the system clipboard (navigator.clipboard.writeText), and burns the media from RAM.

6. Zero Central Server Storage: 100% P2P WebRTC Delivery

When you send a photo or video on HashGANG Chat:

  • Encrypted Browser-to-Browser Pipe: The media is sliced into encrypted binary packets in your browser's RAM and transmitted directly to your stranger's browser using WebRTC RTCDataChannel.
  • Zero Cloud or Disk Footprint: Files are NEVER uploaded to or saved on any HashGANG server. Our signaling server only facilitates initial SDP negotiation and never sees, relays, or stores media binaries.
  • Total Ephemerality: Once closed or when the call ends, device RAM is garbage-collected. There are no persistent URL links, backups, or database records of your photos or videos.
