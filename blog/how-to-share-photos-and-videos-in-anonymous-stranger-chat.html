Sharing a travel photograph, an interesting sketch, a hobby clip, or a funny meme was virtually impossible without surrendering your private phone number or Instagram handle.

HashGANG Chat fundamentally re-engineers this experience. In our latest release (v397), all media shared in 1v1 private conversations is Strictly View-Once by Default. 1. Strictly View-Once by Default: Why Direct View Was Deprecated

On older chat apps, sent media remains pinned in the chat log indefinitely, creating immense anxiety:

  • What if someone walks behind your desk or sits next to you on public transit while a photo is visible?
  • What if a stranger saves, forwards, or re-inspects your photo minutes after the conversation moves on?

To solve this at the root architecture level, HashGANG Chat completely removed unshielded "Direct View" modes from private chat. Every single photo and video uploaded from your camera or gallery is strictly enforced as View-Once:

The Ephemeral View-Once Architecture:

  • Discreet Opaque Cards: Incoming and outgoing media render as discreet status cards in chat: Photo received • [View Photo] or Video received • [View Video]. Nothing is exposed on screen until the user intentionally clicks to view.
  • Single-Use Lifespan: Media can only ever be opened once. The instant the viewing session terminates, the card permanently updates to a grayed-out [1] Opened status. It cannot be re-opened, reloaded, or recovered.
  • Immediate RAM Garbage Collection: As soon as the modal closes, the underlying browser Object Blob URL is immediately revoked (URL.revokeObjectURL), and binary media chunks in device memory are wiped clean (isBurnedInMemory: true).

2. The HTML5 Forensic Lightbox: Timed Photos & Natural Duration Videos

When you tap "View Photo" or "View Video", media opens in an isolated, dark forensic lightbox modal rendered onto an HTML5 <canvas> element with strict timing mechanics:

  • Photos (10-Second Auto-Destruct Countdown): A top progress bar and circular countdown ring smoothly animate from 10 seconds to 0. When the timer expires, the photo instantly closes, burns from memory, and the card transitions to Opened.
  • Videos (Full Natural Duration Guaranteed): Unlike arbitrary timers that prematurely cut off video clips, HashGANG Chat dynamically binds video playback to the media's natural duration. The video plays through once and triggers memory wipe the exact moment it finishes (hiddenVideo.onended).

3. Tri-Point Forensic Watermarking: Live Traceability

To prevent malicious users from capturing uncredited or anonymous media, every single frame rendered in the forensic lightbox is embedded with a high-contrast, semi-transparent Tri-Point Forensic Watermark directly into the canvas bitmap pixels:

Tri-Point Watermark Layout:

  • Top-Left Corner: Displays the authentic brand mark HashGANG at 0.5 opacity with soft drop shadow.
  • Bottom-Right Corner: Displays the viewer's exact localized timestamp including the full year (e.g. Nov 17, 2026, 01:35 PM).
  • Center Stage (The Forensic Core): Displays the viewer's exact anonymous pseudonym and the active 8-character session trace code (e.g. Toothless_87 / 8F3A9X2K) with a soft halo drop shadow. It floats directly over the visual subject without completely obscuring visibility, ensuring that any visual capture links irrevocably to the specific session and viewer.

4. Active Anti-Screenshot Hotkey Interception & Blackout Curtain

HashGANG Chat provides active, proactive client-side defenses while media is displayed in the forensic lightbox:

  • Hotkey Interception: Pressing PrintScreen, Ctrl+P (Print), or Ctrl+S (Save Page) immediately triggers an opaque Security Blackout Curtain, clears the system clipboard (navigator.clipboard.writeText), and burns the media from RAM.
  • Focus Loss & Tab Switch Protection: If the viewer attempts to switch tabs, minimize the browser, or switch to a background screen recording tool, the window blur and document visibilitychange listeners fire instantly, blacking out the screen and terminating the view session permanently.

Many random chat apps make dishonest marketing claims like "100% Screenshot-Proof — Impossible to Record!" At HashGANG, we believe in radical technical honesty and transparency:

Technical Reality vs. Forensic Accountability:

No website, app, or operating system in the world can physically stop someone from pointing a secondary physical smartphone or camera at their computer or phone screen. Any platform claiming otherwise is making a false promise.

However, HashGANG's Tri-Point Forensic Watermarking completely neutralizes the threat of secondary camera leaks:

  • If a bad actor points another phone at their screen and snaps a photo, the resulting image permanently captures their own live username, exact session trace code, and date-time stamp across the media.

6. Zero Central Server Storage: 100% P2P WebRTC Delivery

When you send a photo or video on HashGANG Chat:

  • Encrypted Browser-to-Browser Pipe: The media is sliced into encrypted binary packets in your browser's RAM and transmitted directly to your stranger's browser using WebRTC RTCDataChannel.
  • Zero Cloud or Disk Footprint: Files are NEVER uploaded to or saved on any HashGANG server. Our signaling server only facilitates initial SDP negotiation and never sees, relays, or stores media binaries.
  • Total Ephemerality: Once closed or when the call ends, device RAM is garbage-collected. There are no persistent URL links, backups, or database records of your photos or videos.
