2. How the Technology Works Under the Hood

HashGANG Chat's audio engine is built on compiled high-performance WebAssembly (WASM) and specialized time-domain pitch correction DSP algorithms (leveraging rubberband.wasm and custom SoundTouch audio worklets):

  1. Direct Browser Audio Capture: When you grant microphone access, your browser's MediaStream is piped directly into a dedicated AudioWorkletNode.
  2. Pitch Shifting Without Speed Distortion: Unlike primitive pitch shifting that sounds like a cassette tape played too fast (which distorts tempo), our DSP engine uses high-fidelity phase-vocoder phase preservation. Your speech pace and rhythm stay perfectly natural while the pitch shifts smoothly up or down.
  3. Direct Insertion into WebRTC Mesh: The altered audio buffer is fed directly into the WebRTC peer connection. The stranger on the other end hears your chosen voice instantly in crystal-clear stereo sound.

4. Complete Privacy: Why Client-Side Processing Matters
